VidGrab

Version 1.2.0 · iOS 15 and newer · by T4MAG0

Download videos on your iPhone. Paste a link and pick the quality, or open the built-in browser, play any video and tap the Download button on it.

  • YouTube and lots of other sites, up to 4K when available
  • Built-in browser with an ad blocker
  • Up to 10 downloads at once
  • Saves as MP4 for Photos, or MP3 / M4A for sound only
  • YouTube keeps the original audio language
  • Everything is built in, no extra packages needed

What's new in 1.2.0

  • Now available as a jailbreak package for rootful, rootless and roothide
  • Updates through Sileo
